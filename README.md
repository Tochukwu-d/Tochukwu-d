### 🧭 About Me

I'm a Data Analyst with a background in medical laboratory science, which means I bring a scientist's rigor to data work: hypothesis-driven, detail-obsessed, and comfortable with messy, real-world datasets. I specialize in turning raw transactional and operational data into dashboards and reports that business stakeholders actually use to make decisions — using SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI.

I like problems with a clear business question attached: Where is revenue leaking? Which customers are about to churn? What's driving the variance between forecast and actual? My projects below are built around answering questions like that, end-to-end.
